Yes, there are some banks in the United States that do not charge a fee for incoming wire transfers. However, it is important to note that while the bank may not charge a fee for the transfer, the sender's bank or the intermediary bank may still charge a fee for sending the wire. In addition, some banks may have minimum account balance requirements or other account-specific conditions that must be met in order to receive incoming wire transfers without a fee.It is always a good idea to check with the bank directly to confirm its policies on incoming wire transfer fees and any other conditions that may apply. You can also shop around and compare the fees and policies of different banks to find one that meets your needs.
