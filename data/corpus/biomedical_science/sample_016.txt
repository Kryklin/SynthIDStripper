It's generally not advisable for children under the age of 12 to participate in long-distance running, such as four miles, due to the potential for overuse injuries and the stress it can place on their developing bodies. Children's bodies are still growing and developing, and participating in activities that involve high impact and repetitive movements can increase their risk of injury. It's important to encourage children to be physically active, but it's also important to ensure that they are participating in activities that are appropriate for their age and development level.

That being said, every child is different and some children may be more physically able than others. If your son is able to complete the runs without experiencing pain or exhaustion and is enjoying them, it's possible that he may be able to continue participating in these activities. However, it's important to monitor his progress and pay attention to any changes in his ability or willingness to participate. If you have any concerns about your son's participation in these activities, it's always a good idea to consult with a healthcare provider.
